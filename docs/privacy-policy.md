## 3. Personal information we collect

We collect personal information directly from you, from your organization, from
other users in your organization, from service providers, and automatically when
you use LockStock.

### Account and authentication information

We collect information used to create, secure, and manage accounts, including:

- Name.
- Email address.
- Password or authentication credentials processed through our authentication
  provider.
- Company or organization name.
- Account confirmation status.
- Authentication tokens, session information, and access metadata.

### Organization, role, and invitation information

We collect information used to manage access to organizations and teams,
including:

- Organization names.
- Team names and descriptions.
- Member user IDs, names, email addresses, roles, and creation dates.
- Invitation email addresses, invitation status, invited-by identifiers,
  accepted-by identifiers, token hashes, expiration dates, and acceptance dates.
- Role changes and membership activity.

### Inventory and purchasing information

Customers and users may submit operational records that include personal
information, including:

- Material names, SKUs, descriptions, categories, units of measure, quantities,
  and minimum stock thresholds.
- Location names, codes, addresses, warehouse information, site information, and
  active status.
- Supplier or vendor names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, lead
  times, payment terms, tax or registration details if entered, and activity
  status.
- Purchase order numbers, expected dates, notes, statuses, line items, prices,
  currencies, sent dates, received dates, and receiving details.
- Stock movement notes, reasons, references, quantities, locations, and
  creator information.
- CSV import content submitted by users.

### Audit, report, and usage information

We collect information used to provide security, reporting, and activity
history, including:

- Audit log entries for created, updated, and deleted records.
- Actor user IDs, entity types, entity IDs, labels, messages, metadata, and

- Low-stock alert and stock health report inputs and outputs.
- Workflow, page, and feature interactions where analytics or logs are enabled.
