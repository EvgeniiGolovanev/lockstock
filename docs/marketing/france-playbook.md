# France SME acquisition playbook

This internal playbook defines how LockStock tests acquisition with French
small and medium-sized businesses during the first 90 days. Keep this document
internal. The public campaign pages contain only customer-facing content.

## Scope and objective

The campaign tests which SME segment produces qualified trials and demo
requests at a repeatable cost. Start with three segments:

- Construction companies that manage materials across sites and depots.
- Light industrial companies and workshops that manage parts and consumables.
- Field maintenance teams that distribute parts across sites and vehicles.

Exclude TikTok from the initial channel mix. Focus resources on search, SEO,
LinkedIn, targeted B2B outreach, partnerships, events, and software directories.

## Channel strategy

Measure every channel by segment and connect each activity to a specific
landing page or offer.

### Google Search Ads

Use paid search to capture existing demand for stock, inventory, Excel
replacement, and supplier management.

- Create ad groups for Excel replacement, construction sites, inventory, and
  supplier orders.
- Send each segment to its dedicated landing page.
- Exclude searches about jobs, training, school definitions, and permanently
  free software.

### French SEO

Build durable qualified traffic around operational stock and purchasing use
cases.

- Publish pillar pages for SME inventory, Excel replacement, supplier orders,
  and multi-site stock.
- Answer specific operational questions with concise supporting articles.
- Link each article to the free trial, demo request, and stock-audit checklist.

### LinkedIn

Reach operations, purchasing, supply-chain, and SME leadership roles through
organic posts and tightly targeted paid campaigns.

- Publish founder posts with product screenshots, workflows, and common Excel
  failure modes.
- Retarget visitors to demo, pricing, and checklist pages after consent.
- Limit lead-generation campaigns to relevant operations, purchasing, and
  leadership roles.

### Targeted B2B outreach

Test segments quickly with role-relevant contact lists and concise messages.

- Build lists by region, industry, and professional role.
- Include a business problem, a relevant product capability, and a simple
  opt-out in every email.
- Use manual LinkedIn follow-up after meaningful engagement.

### Partnerships and events

Generate qualified introductions through advisers, integrators, and SME
networks.

- Run webinars with operations consultants, adjacent Odoo or Sage specialists,
  chambers of commerce, and supply-chain experts.
- Book meetings around industry and logistics events before buying exhibition
  space.
- Define a referral commission for partners that introduce qualified accounts.

### Directories and reviews

Build evidence for prospects comparing inventory-management products.

- Maintain Appvizer, Capterra, and GetApp listings with current screenshots and
  a product demo.
- Ask active users for a review after they complete their first workflow.
- Reuse verified reviews on segment pages and in nurture emails.

## Messaging sequences

Keep messages short, operational, and useful. Preserve the French copy below
when running the sequences, and adapt only the segment-specific context.

### Cold B2B outreach

Target operations, purchasing, stock, construction, maintenance, and SME
leadership roles.

1. **Vos stocks sont encore suivis dans Excel ?**

   Bonjour, je contacte les equipes qui gerent stocks, achats et fournisseurs
   avec plusieurs fichiers Excel. LockStock aide a centraliser articles,
   emplacements, commandes fournisseurs et mouvements. Si ce sujet n'est pas
   pertinent pour votre role, repondez desinscription et je ne vous
   recontacterai pas.

2. **Exemple concret: stock bas + commande fournisseur**

   Un cas frequent: l'equipe voit trop tard qu'un article critique passe sous
   seuil minimum, puis ne sait pas si une commande fournisseur est deja
   ouverte. LockStock relie seuils, emplacements, fournisseurs, bons de
   commande et receptions dans le meme espace.

3. **Audit stock PME en 15 minutes**

   Je peux vous envoyer une checklist courte pour identifier les points
   faibles de votre suivi stock actuel: fichiers multiples, seuils, mouvements,
   receptions, droits d'acces et audit.

### Free-trial nurture

Target contacts who create an account or request the stock-audit checklist.

1. **Commencez par 10 articles critiques**

   Pour evaluer LockStock, importez d'abord les articles qui provoquent le plus
   de ruptures ou d'achats urgents. Ajoutez un emplacement, un fournisseur et
   un seuil minimum.

2. **Reliez achats et receptions**

   Creez un bon de commande fournisseur, marquez-le envoye, puis receptionnez
   partiellement ou totalement les lignes. Vous obtenez un historique clair
   entre achat et stock.

3. **Controlez les roles et l'audit**

   Invitez une personne de l'equipe et verifiez les droits. Les operations
   importantes restent visibles dans le journal d'activite et l'export d'audit
   selon le plan.

### Demo follow-up

Target prospects who request a demo or visit the pricing page.

1. **Preparer la demo LockStock**

   Pour rendre la demo utile, envoyez simplement votre contexte: nombre
   d'articles, emplacements, fournisseurs, utilisateurs et principal probleme
   actuel.

2. **Ce que nous couvrirons en 20 minutes**

   La demo suit un scenario simple: catalogue articles, stock par emplacement,
   commande fournisseur, reception, mouvement de stock, roles et audit.

### Inactive-trial reactivation

Target trial accounts that don't complete an activation workflow after several
days.

1. **Besoin d'aide pour importer vos articles ?**

   Si l'essai est bloque par la preparation du fichier, commencez avec 10
   lignes seulement: SKU, nom, unite et stock minimum. Vous pourrez enrichir
   ensuite.

2. **Votre premier workflow stock en 5 minutes**

   Creez un emplacement, ajoutez un article, enregistrez un mouvement et
   consultez l'etat du stock. Ce parcours suffit pour verifier si LockStock
   correspond a votre equipe.

## Weekly measurement

Track the complete funnel by channel and segment. Review these metrics every
week:

- Visits.
- Trials.
- Demo requests.
- Qualified leads.
- Activation.
- Paid conversion.
- Estimated customer acquisition cost.

Treat a channel as promising only when it produces qualified trials or demo
requests at a cost that can be repeated.

## CNIL and GDPR safeguards

Apply these safeguards before launching paid retargeting or outbound activity:

- Obtain consent before using personalized advertising and retargeting
  trackers.
- Keep B2B outreach relevant to the professional role contacted.
- Include a simple opt-out in every outbound sequence.
- Maintain a CRM suppression list so opted-out contacts aren't contacted
  again.
- Record each contact's source, target segment, and processing purpose.

## Customer case-study template

Use this structure during customer interviews and when preparing verified
social proof:

1. **Problem:** Describe the primary operational risk, such as a stockout,
   urgent purchase, missing stock, or unreliable spreadsheet.
2. **Previous workflow:** Record how the team used spreadsheets, messages,
   calls, and manual corrections.
3. **LockStock workflow:** Show the item, location, supplier, purchase order,
   receipt, stock movement, and audit flow.
4. **Measured result:** Report a concrete result, such as time saved, stockouts
   avoided, clearer orders, or active users.

## Next steps

Run the campaign in measured increments:

- Launch one search and one targeted outreach test per segment.
- Review funnel metrics weekly.
- Keep the strongest segment and message after the first meaningful sample.
- Replace planning assumptions with verified customer evidence.
